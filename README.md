<h3 align = 'center'>Hospitalizations Due To Various Pollutants</h3>
<h4 align = 'center'>Caitlyn Van and Jeraldine Rivas-Calderon</h4>
<h5>Project Description</h5>
<p>This data science project aims to understand how air quality in major cities affects citizens' health and which respiratory diseases may be associated with different pollutants. We want to study various factors that may or may not have impacts on pollutant levels that would subsequently have effects on the health of people. Factors include location relative to a major city, the type of harmful element polluting the air, and the amount of the element. In addition to analyzing the air quality data we collect, we will also try to predict how many individuals are affected by pollution based on historical data. 
</p>
